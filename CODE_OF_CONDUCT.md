# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to fostering a welcoming and respectful environment for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behaviour include:

- Being respectful and considerate.
- Accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on collaboration and learning.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour include:

- Harassment or discrimination.
- Personal attacks or offensive language.
- Any behaviour that creates an unwelcoming environment.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers reserve the right to remove contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.
